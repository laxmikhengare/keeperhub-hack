stale-threshold

agreement 80% not_ready ABORTED

The new workflow systematically misses settlements at the exact deadline due to a strict inequality check (> instead of >=), compounded by a stale cached deadline. With a tolerance window of 12 seconds (one block at this chain's 12s/block rate), these are not timing delays—they are obligation failures. Three of six disagreements are regressions (observations 12, 17, 22), each a missed epoch settlement that cannot be recovered. Do not cut over until the deadline comparison logic is corrected to accept action at the deadline, not only after it.

Blocking

  • New workflow compares timestamp > deadline instead of >= deadline, systematically missing settlements that arrive exactly at the deadline. With a 12-second tolerance window (one block), there is no margin to recover; every miss is a degraded epoch that cannot be retried.
  • Cached deadline is not properly refreshed after epoch settlement, causing subsequent blocks to trigger on stale deadlines. This cascades from each missed settlement into further incorrect state.
  • Root cause must be identified and corrected: either the comparison operator or the cache invalidation logic (or both). The current implementation will record every epoch settlement as missed until fixed.

  • benign_old_keeper_wasteful block 11475634

  • regression block 11475638

    Settlement deadline exactly 1786567848; new workflow declines because timestamp is not strictly past. Third instance of the same failure: the new workflow has a strict-inequality bug in its deadline comparison. Every scheduled deadline is missed by this logic. With 12-second tolerance and one block per 12 seconds, every miss is fatal.
